Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: static Unity 2.5D platform-fighter background, 16:9 landscape, for a camera that often zooms into the centre
> Primary request: Create a moderately detailed replacement background for an abandoned ecological research reserve 150 years in the future. It must be richer than a mostly empty blue-sky backdrop, but much simpler and calmer than a dense futuristic city or megastructure.
> Scene/backdrop: A peaceful reclaimed highland valley above clouds. Place one medium-sized ruined ecological research observatory in the distant centre: a broken partial ring around a slender cream-colored tower, softened by atmospheric haze and overgrown with vegetation. Add only two small secondary details: one distant greenhouse dome and one broken elevated rail segment. Surround them with layered low-poly mountains, forest terraces, a few wind turbines, cloud bands, and scattered geometric trees. No other major buildings.
> Style/medium: clean stylized 3D low-poly game environment, broad simple shapes, flat-shaded geometry, crisp silhouettes, limited surface detail, cohesive with bright grass-and-earth platform models
> Composition/framing: straight-on wide side view. Keep visible environmental detail in the central 50 percent so zoomed framing is not empty, but maintain generous breathing room around every structure. The central observatory occupies no more than 20 percent of image width and sits in the middle distance. Use mountains, clouds, and forest layers rather than architecture to fill most of the image. Keep the bottom centre quiet for the main stage and keep structures behind the combat plane.
> Lighting/mood: bright comfortable late-morning daylight, peaceful hopeful post-collapse mood, soft haze, gentle shadows
> Color palette: pale blue, mint green, warm cream, muted stone grey, restrained cyan indicator lights
> Constraints: background only; no characters, UI, text, logos, watermark, weapons, fire, temples, recognizable game IP, foreground stage, or horizontal objects that resemble playable platforms. Central scenery must remain readable but low contrast enough for white and red fighters.
> Avoid: dense city, giant dominant rings, many towers, clutter, excessive floating islands, dramatic cinematic ruins, photorealism, painterly rendering, blank featureless sky
