Responsive longsword and evasion prototype (2026-10-26)
Reference: https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1EY41157Ep/?p=4
Uses the project's existing sword clips and five licensed clips from the supplied
Runner Action Animation Pack 1.4. No animation data was extracted from the video.

Current overworld HeroActions library enables responsiveSwordCombat.
LMB: three-cut chain; tap again during an attack to buffer the next cut.
Tap Shift or RMB + direction: short grounded evasion on release; no direction retreats.
Hold Shift or RMB for 0.20s: sprint directly, without a leading dodge.
After 0.45s without attacking/guarding, sword rests visibly across the back.
A fresh attack draws from the back in 0.12s, before the first damage window.
Hold direction after the evasion to resume locomotion; hold sprint to run.
An attack pressed in the evasion's final portion is buffered into a cut.
The committed active sword swing cannot be erased; startup/recovery can evade.
V also uses short evasion; the legacy roll has no player input binding.
Perfect dodge: enemy speed 0.16x, automatic approach, hold/tap LMB for up to
six cuts, then retreat along the approach path to the pre-rush position.
Attacks turn toward nearby visible enemies before the active sword window.

A1/A2/A3: 0.62/0.46/0.76 seconds. Their chain junctions are 0.46/0.32/0.64.
Directional evasion: 0.42 seconds, 2.4 metre collision-controlled travel by 0.31.
Evasion-to-attack unlock: 0.27; movement unlock: 0.31; cooldown: 0.46.
Damage is still sampled along the displayed sword and deduplicated per action.
This is a timing and movement prototype, not the full Naraka combat ruleset.

Editor menus: Tools/Hero/Apply responsive sword prototype,
Check responsive sword prototype, Render responsive sword poses,
Play and verify responsive sword. Play verification uses QA.NativePlay private
save scope and restores the normal combat input reader when finished.

Link time presentation: shared runtime URP grading/bloom/chromatic edge,
procedural radial screen shader, energy rings, crystals and impact bursts,
bounded spectral mesh afterimages, luminous blade trail, existing audio cues.
All presentation uses the player clock, cleans up on return/disable, and never
owns damage. Return retraces the collision-checked path at up to 32 m/s.

Death revision: compared original death and CLazy StandDie / StandDie_Root /
TurnDie on the actual hero. Selected non-looping StandDie, 1.193s collapse,
then its own final pose. Original clip held the body about 0.92m above ground.
A 120Hz skin-height calibration is paired to this clip for grounded death only.
Air death retains motor gravity and switches to collapse after real contact.
